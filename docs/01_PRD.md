# PareFood Product Requirements Document
Version 1.0 — Complex MVP

## 1. Product Vision
Build a complete local food-ordering and delivery operating system that can run real merchants, customers, drivers, payments, deliveries, promotions, support, reporting, and settlements in one controlled platform.

## 2. MVP philosophy
This is a complex MVP, not a toy MVP. It must cover the complete operational loop while avoiding premature national-scale complexity.

## 9. Success criteria
A real customer can:
discover → select food → checkout → pay → merchant accepts → food prepared → driver assigned → pickup verified → delivery completed → customer reviews.

A real operator can observe and intervene at every critical stage.

## 10. Out of scope for first production MVP
- Multi-country
- Multi-currency
- Marketplace for non-food goods
- Complex loyalty ecosystem
- Franchise hierarchy
- AI dispatch optimization
- Wallet with stored customer funds
- Cryptocurrency
